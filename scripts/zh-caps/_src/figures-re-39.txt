re-39-idioms-by-level	"Blue rows are the division and the modulo by 10, amber rows are the switch lowering, green rows are the vectorized loop, purple rows are the branchless select. Rows with no tint are prologue, loop control, or moves the recognizer does not claim. On x86-64 at -O0 the division is already a magic multiply, the modulo is a hardware idivl, and the switch is a jump table. On arm64 at -O0 both use sdiv and the switch is a compare chain. At -O1 the switch becomes a lookup table on both. At -O2 and -O3 the loop is vectorized, the x86-64 switch is a jump table again, and the arm64 switch is a comparison tree."

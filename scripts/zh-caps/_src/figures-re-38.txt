re-38-three-frames	"The black line is the stack pointer at entry. On x86-64 the return address sits just above it. Cells above the line belong to the caller: stack arguments and, on Microsoft x64, the 32-byte shadow space. Cells below the line are the callee's frame: saved registers, the frame record on AArch64, spilled argument registers at -O0, and the amber dashed box is the System V red zone when the function uses it."
